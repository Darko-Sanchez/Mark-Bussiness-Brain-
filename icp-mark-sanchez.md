## SECTION 3: THE CORE PROBLEM THEY WANT SOLVED

### For Restaurant Groups:
**Problem 1:** Systems are in place but the experience feels hollow — technically correct service that guests tolerate rather than remember.
**Problem 2:** Team culture depends on whoever is strongest on the floor that night. No replicable standard for human connection.

### For Independent Owners:
**Problem 1:** No systems, no structure. Chaos. The owner is the glue holding everything together.
**Problem 2:** Can't step away — the restaurant quality drops visibly when they're not there.
**Problem 3:** Reviews tell the truth. "Food's good, service is slow" is the pattern.

**The data makes this concrete:** At Booty's Wings, when one strong performer left, the 5-star rate dropped from 92% to 59% and negative reviews jumped from 2.9% to 26.6% — a 9x increase in dissatisfaction. That is what owner-dependency and no transferable standard looks like in public data.

### For Hospitality Professionals:
**Problem 1:** Overgiving and emotional burnout without knowing how to manage it.
**Problem 2:** Stuck in an industry they're not sure they want forever, with no roadmap forward.
**Problem 3:** Not realizing that the skill set they have is genuinely valuable and measurably rare.

**Why they haven't solved it:** They've tried doing it alone — books, podcasts, self-implementation. They may have hired someone who didn't understand their real problem. What's been missing is someone who understands the human and connection side of the work, not just operational surface fixes.
